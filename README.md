# 🏏 STRYKO

### Your Cricket. Your Score. Your Game.

STRYKO is a cricket scoring app designed to make scoring your matches simple, convenient and enjoyable.

Whether you're playing with friends, organising a local match or keeping track of your game, STRYKO brings scoring to your fingertips.
